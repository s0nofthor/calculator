# calculator
A calculator for simple equations with a running memory that can easily be referenced.

How To Use:
This functions like a regular calculator, all you need to do is type the equations on your keyboard or click the buttons on the calculator on screen to type. It also logs the equations entered so you can retrieve them when you click on them, displayed on a "chalkboard".

Biggest Challenges:

While I still am struggling with time management as far as dedicating the necessary hours to create things, I do feel that this is my most fully realized project to date and look forward to making less creative concessions while picking up my pace as I move forward.
